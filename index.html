HMS Javelin
HMS Javelin was a J-class destroyer built by John Brown & Company and commissioned in June 1939. She displaced 1,690 long tons and carried six 4.7-inch guns in three turrets plus twelve secondary guns. In November 1940, while on patrol in the English Channel, she was torpedoed by the German destroyer Z10. Both her bow and stern were blown off, leaving only 155 feet of her original 356-foot hull still afloat. She was towed back to port and fully repaired by late 1941.
